Five built-in AI capabilities

Automating the tedious, simplifying the complex, and optimizing the invisible.

1. SQL AI Functions

Bring LLMs directly into your SQL editor. Use ai_classify, ai_generate, and ai_complete to analyze sentiment or classify text right in your query.

  • No Python Required
  • Unstructured Data Analysis

2. Integrated AI Agent

More than just a code generator. A proactive copilot that helps you discover data, visualizes trends, and optimizes your SQL queries conversationally.

3. AI-Generated Metadata

Let the Lakehouse document itself. Generative AI automatically creates wikis, descriptions, and tags for your datasets, building trust and context.

4. Autonomous Performance

Self-optimizing engine. Autonomous Reflections and Result Caching learn from your workload to speed up queries without manual tuning.

5. Extensible AI (MCP)

Use AI your way. Connect ChatGPT or Claude to your data using the open Model Context Protocol (MCP). Democratize access for every persona.
